What we do

About

Two things, in parallel. We teach machine learning properly, starting from fundamentals and working up through current practice, so that someone who has never trained a model can follow. And we build, on projects with real users, real datasets, and real constraints.

Members come from Columbia College, SEAS, Barnard and GS. No prior research experience is expected. Teams stay small so that everyone writes code rather than watching someone else write it.
