See our projects → Join us →

What we do

Our education program takes students from no ML experience to building and deploying production ML systems. Through workshops and hands-on projects, students learn the fundamentals, train and evaluate models, and build the pipelines and infrastructure needed to run them. Along the way, they work in small teams on student ideas and projects with industry partners.

Open to undergraduate and graduate students across Columbia, including Columbia College, SEAS, Barnard, and GS. No prior ML experience needed.
